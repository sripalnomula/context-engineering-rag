Safety warnings

Disconnect the battery and wait at least 90 seconds before opening the motor-controller enclosure. Capacitors can retain a hazardous charge after the display turns off.

Firmware update

Keep charge above 40 percent during an update. Revision 7.1 requires updater application 4.6 or later. If an update stops before verification, leave the scooter powered on and contact service engineering; do not disconnect the battery.
