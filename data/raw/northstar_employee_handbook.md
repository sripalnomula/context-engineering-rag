# Northstar Mobility Employee Handbook

Document ID: `handbook-2026`  
Version: 3.2  
Effective date: 2026-01-15  
Owner: People Operations

## Hybrid work

Employees assigned to the Seattle office may work remotely up to **three days per week**. Tuesday is the common collaboration day and requires in-office attendance unless a director approves an exception in writing. Employees who live more than 80 miles from a Northstar office are classified as remote-first and are not subject to the Tuesday requirement.

### International remote work

International remote work is limited to **20 calendar days per year**. Requests must be submitted to People Operations at least 30 days before travel. Access to production customer data is prohibited while working internationally, even through the corporate VPN.

## Travel and expenses

Expense reports are due within **15 calendar days** after a trip ends. Receipts are mandatory for any single expense of **$50 or more**. Managers may approve domestic hotel rates up to $275 per night; higher rates require Finance approval.

Flights under six scheduled hours must be booked in economy. Premium economy is allowed for flights from six through nine hours. Business class requires a scheduled flight longer than nine hours and vice-president approval.

## Learning budget

Every full-time employee receives a **$1,500 annual learning allowance**. Certifications, technical books, and role-relevant conferences qualify. Laptops, phones, and recurring entertainment subscriptions do not qualify. Unused funds expire on December 31 and do not roll over.

## Security incidents

Suspected credential compromise must be reported to the Security Operations Center within **30 minutes** using the `#security-urgent` channel or the 24-hour hotline. Do not reset or power off a potentially compromised corporate device until Security provides instructions, because doing so may destroy volatile evidence.
